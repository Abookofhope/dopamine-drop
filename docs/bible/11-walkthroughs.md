# 11. Walkthroughs: early, mid and late

Three sessions in the life of one player, Sam, in the finished overhaul. Each
step names the system at work and the pillar it serves, so the walkthrough
doubles as a test script.

## Early: day 1, the first 10 minutes

**0:00.** Sam opens the link on the bus. The welcome card: the cat-drop logo
blinks, one line says "44 small puzzles; the one in front of you changes every
round", and a big button says **Start your first run**. *(Minimal reading:
onboarding in play.)*

**0:04.** The count-in rings, then Odd Skein: nine skeins, one a shade off. Sam
taps it. Soft tick, the skein glows, a burst of yarn, two warm notes, a 16 ms
buzz. **Solved.** *(T2. Competence in four seconds.)*

**0:09.** Count the Stitches: tap 1, 2, 3, 4. Each tap plucks a note a third
higher. **Clean**: three warm notes, glints along the board. "Clean"
appears under the score for a moment. *(First grade, taught by showing.)*

**0:20.** A miss on Dye Trap. A gentle snag ghost, a falling tone, "−4s", and
the line says "the word, not the colour". No shake, no red screen. The next
board deals. *(Failure that informs. Safety.)*

**0:45.** Heat ×2: a ring around the multiplier, and the music's soft piano
layer joins the pad that has been there all along. *(Sensory, flow.)*

**1:10.** Swift and Clean on Quick Count: **Purrfect**. A gold stamp, three
rising notes that fit the chord, a double buzz, and a knot pops on a strand of
yarn across the top of the frame. *(T4, the Thread introduced by
appearing.)*

**3:30.** A stage beat after 10 solves: light sweeps, stars fountain, and three
charm cards are dealt. Sam picks Yarnball. *(Autonomy, a first build
choice.)*

**9:58.** The clock runs out. Game Over leads with **"Purrfect on Quick Count,
1.8 s"** and replays its chime, then the score counts up, the XP bar fills,
and 31 yarn flies to the basket. "Next up: 19 more yarn for Noodle." The Room
is called once. *(Peak-End.)*

**10:20.** In the Room, Biscuit is riding along. The quilt on the back wall
has nine patches knitted in and thirty-five dashed outlines. Sam taps one
patch and sees "Played once. Play it in Cozy to practise." *(Ownership seeded,
the long game shown.)*

## Mid: day 12, a 15-minute evening session (about 5 hours played)

**Arrival.** The daily gift (day 5 of the ladder, because Sam missed
Saturday and it **rested** rather than reset): 50 yarn. A wish is waiting:
"Tie 3 Threads." *(Nothing lost for a day off.)*

**The door.** Tired today, Sam flips the switch to **Cozy** and taps Play a mix
on Calm & Collect. No clock. The music station is Hearth: slower, warmer.
*(Autonomy, Bliss.)*

**Minutes 1–8.** Patchwork, Latch Hook, Follow the Thread, and so on. A miss
on Latch Hook just cools the heat a step, and the Thread holds. Two Bows: the
strand loops into a bow over the board, the pad swells and +5 yarn each time. *(Flow without pressure.)*

**Minute 9.** Ten solves: a stage gift instead of a draft, 10 yarn, a soft
sweep. *(Landmarks without decisions.)*

**Minute 12.** Sam taps Pause, then **End the run**. The recap leads with
"Two Bows". Then: 38 solves, 74 yarn, a quilt stitch on Latch Hook
(first Swift), Sesame's bond reaches 2 ("Sesame's passive steps up"), and the
wish ticked. *(Progress you can see.)*

**Minute 13.** **Tuck in** is offered beside Back to menu. Sam taps it: the
Room dims to evening, Biscuit and Sesame curl up on the bed, the radio thins
to a music box, and a single line says "Goodnight. Your quilt has 14 patches."
After 8 seconds, the menu. *(Restoration: the five-minute test passed on a
fifteen-minute evening.)*

## Late: month 3, an hour on a Sunday (about 40 hours played)

**The quilt.** 41 patches knitted, 23 with three rows, 6 with gold borders,
**2 Gold Threads** (Odd Skein and Snip). The fairy-lights and music-box
milestones are on the shelf. The 1,000-solve fireplace glows on the floor in
the evening light. *(Ownership: this room can only be Sam's.)*

**The Kit.** Sam switches to the "Speedrun" kit: Pixel and Truffle riding
along (a friend pair), Zeal 3 and Rally 2, the Starlight sparkle, the Busy
Paws station. *(Loadout, style.)*

**Challenge.** Sam plays Quick Paws in Challenge. The ghost thread under the
score shows last week's best: Sam is 340 behind at 2:00. *(Mastery against
oneself.)*

**Minute 6.** A hot streak: ×5 (the room's bells come in), and a Thread closing
into a Bow *during* a stage beat. The biggest moment the game
makes: cradle, sweep, swell and the charm draft deals a rare card. The ghost
flips to gold: "+120". *(T5 stacked into T6. Earned spectacle.)*

**Minute 10.** New best. Game Over leads with it, and the near-miss bar from
before is replaced by the gold stamp. The Style profile: Swift 61%, Clean 48%,
Purrfect 29%. "Try Clean on Mending: it's your slowest." *(Informative
mastery.)*

**Afterwards.** Sam shares the Daily Drop result. The message carries a ghost
link. A friend opens it that evening and races Sam's thread on the same
twelve boards. No accounts, no leaderboard. *(Light, inspiring social.)*

**The Archive.** It's winter, and the Snowfall kit and a knitted-stocking
decor set are in the shop. Sam also buys last autumn's Lantern from the
Archive at its original price. *(Seasons without FOMO.)*

## What each walkthrough proves

| Walkthrough | Checks |
|---|---|
| Early | first solve under 5 s; grades and the Thread taught by showing; Game Over leads with a peak; the Room and the quilt are seen in session one |
| Mid | a missed day costs nothing; Cozy is a full game; Tuck in ends on a high; mid-term progress (bond, quilt, wish) is listed in the recap |
| Late | the long tail (Gold Threads, milestones, seasons) still has new depth at 40 hours; loadouts express style; social is optional and pressure-free |
