# 4. Progression, collection and ownership

Ownership is the outer loop's reason to exist. The player should feel that
this room, these cats and this quilt could only be theirs, because they hold
the history of how *they* played. Each system below says which pillar it
serves and what makes it permanent.

## Three time scales

| Scale | Live today | Added by the overhaul | Pillars |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Session** (one run, minutes) | score, streak heat, stage beats, charm draft, new best | style grades, the Thread and Cat's Cradle, Patchwork, the run's peak on Game Over, the Today card | Flow, Competence, Peak-End |
| **Day / week** | Daily Drop, 3 wishes a day, 9 a week, the daily gift | a gift that rests instead of resetting, a **Weekly Pattern** (one featured twist and one featured game, with a quilt stitch as the reward and no deadline on keeping it), the Daily **ghost link** | Progress, Autonomy, Relatedness |
| **Lifetime** (weeks to months) | level, perks, stars and mastery ranks, cats and bonds, the Room, achievements | **the Quilt**, **Milestones** that permanently change the room, the **Gold Thread**, Sparkle kits, Room Radio stations, Seasons and the Archive | Ownership, Mastery |

## The Quilt (new, the centrepiece)

A quilt with one patch for each of the 44 games hangs on the Room's back wall,
growing over the player's lifetime. It is the most visible record of how this
player has played, and nobody else's quilt looks the same.

| Patch state | Rule | Look |
|---|---|---|
| Empty | never played | a dashed outline in the family colour, the game's glyph faint |
| Tried | played at least once | a plain knitted patch in the family colour, the glyph stitched on |
| Stars 1–3 | Classic Marathon stars | one, two, three rows of running stitch across the patch |
| Rank | mastery rank (Kitten to Lion) | a border: none, cotton, wool, silk, velvet, **gold** for Lion |
| Gold Thread | Lion rank **and** a Purrfect rate of 50% or more over the game's last 20 solves | the glyph re-stitched in gold, with a slow glint |

- **Tap a patch**: the game's card with its best, stars, rank, Purrfect rate,
  twists unlocked, and **Play** (Marathon) and **Practise** (Cozy) buttons. The
  quilt is also a way into a game.
- **New stitch moment** (T5): after a run that changed a patch, the Room shows
  the patch stitching itself in the next time it opens: a needle and thread
  on the canvas, a soft thud, a purr.
- **Why it works:** endowment (the quilt is unique and visibly hard-won),
  competence (every stitch is a real achievement), autonomy (any order),
  a long tail (132 stars, 44 ranks, 44 gold threads is months of goals with no
  timers).

## Milestones (new): lifetime numbers that change the room forever

Permanent beauty, never power. Each milestone adds something to the Room scene
the first time it is reached, with a T6 moment the next time the Room opens.

| Milestone | Unlock |
|---|---|
| 100 solves | fairy lights along the window (twinkle as a moment, not a loop) |
| 500 solves | a music box on the shelf: tap it to switch Room Radio stations |
| 1,000 solves | a fireplace glow on the floor in the evening |
| 2,500 solves | a moon and stars in the window at night |
| 5,000 solves | a yarn-ball mobile above the bed |
| 10,000 solves | a constellation in the shape of your first cat on the ceiling |
| 10 Purrfects in one run | a gold frame on the quilt |
| 7-day Daily streak (live: Comet) | Comet is adoptable (live) |
| every game tried | the quilt gets its backing and hanging rod |
| every game at Lion | the quilt is lit by a lamp of its own |

## Cats (live, deepened)

Live: 20 cats, a passive each in 3 steps, bonds at 60, 300 and 900 solves
ridden, 10 friend pairs, up to 3 seats.

Added:
- **Style reactions** at bond 1: the riding cat reacts on the HUD to Purrfects
  (ears up), the Thread closing (stands and stretches) and a miss (a slow
  blink, never a frown).
- **The 900 bond** gives more than a gold bow: the cat gets a **name tag the
  player writes** (12 characters, kept on the device). A named cat is the
  strongest ownership object a cozy game can offer.
- **Accessories** (a sink, cosmetic): collars, bows and scarves, three per cat
  at 150, 300 and 600 yarn, shown in the Room and on the HUD mascot.

## Loadout: "Your kit" (new)

One screen, reached from the Room, gathers the choices that make a run
*yours*:

| Slot | Choices | Changes | Since |
|---|---|---|---|
| Riding along | 1–3 cats | passives, friend pairs (power, earned with yarn) | live |
| Perks | slots earned with lifetime points | 6 perks × 3 levels (power, earned by play) | live |
| Sparkle | victory colour (live, 6) → **Sparkle kits** | the palette and accent shape of the solve spray, the token and the grade stamp (cosmetic) | new |
| Station | Room Radio stations | the music's instruments and tempo (cosmetic) | new |

Kits are presets: three saved kits, named by the player ("Cozy evening",
"Speedrun"), one tap to switch. Skill expression comes from choosing cats and
perks that fit how you play, then seeing it in your stats.

## Mastery and skill expression

- **Style profile** on the You tab: your Clean, Swift and Purrfect rates over
  the last 100 solves, as three bars. It shows what kind of player you are
  ("mostly Swift: try Clean on Patchwork") without ranking you against
  anyone.
- **Per-game numbers** on the quilt card: best, stars, rank, Purrfect rate, your
  fastest solve.
- **Twists** (live: Blitz, Fog, Frenzy, Ironclad) stay the deep end. The
  overhaul adds no new twist. Their stars feed the quilt's rows.

## Rules for anything new in this chapter

1. **Permanent.** Nothing earned is ever taken back (the existing reset keeps
   yarn, cats and room, and also keeps the quilt and milestones).
2. **Visible.** Every unlock appears somewhere the player looks at least once a
   session: the Room, the HUD or the recap.
3. **Earnable by any player.** Cosmetics never require Challenge play. Skill
   unlocks (the Gold Thread, stars) are badges of skill, not cosmetics
   withheld from Cozy players.
4. **Explained.** Every locked thing says exactly how to get it.
